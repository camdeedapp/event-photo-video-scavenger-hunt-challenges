# Wedding Event Scavenger Hunt & Photo Challenge Ideas  

**Use these prompts to make your wedding interactive, fun, and memorable.**

### Challenge Prompts
1. “Snap a selfie with someone who travelled the farthest to be here.”  
2. “Capture the couple’s parents sharing their best dance move.”  
3. “Film the moment the newlyweds cut their cake — get the best reaction shot.”  
4. “Create a 5-second video message for the newlyweds’ digital guestbook.”  
5. “Find someone using their phone to create a ‘wedding guest book’ feel — capture it!”  
6. “Take a photo with the wedding photo album display and tag your favourite moment.”  
7. “Use the QR code to upload your photo and instantly join the live leaderboard.”  
8. “Guess the song the couple will dance to next — submit your guess via Camdeed.”

### Why It Works
These prompts leverage guests’ enthusiasm and technology — scan the QR code, upload instantly, join a live game of photo and video sharing, and help build a meaningful wedding guest book and photo archive.
