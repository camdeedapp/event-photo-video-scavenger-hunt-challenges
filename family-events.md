# Family & Friends Gathering Challenge Prompts  

**For birthdays, reunions, anniversaries, or casual celebrations.**

### Challenge Prompts
1. “Find someone who hasn’t seen the birthday person in more than 5 years — take a selfie!”  
2. “Use your phone to upload a video message for the guest of honour’s digital guestbook.”  
3. “Capture a photo with your favourite childhood game at the gathering.”  
4. “Find a group of three different generations and take a fun photo.”  
5. “Guess the oldest family photo you know — upload a snap from your phone.”  
6. “Use the QR code to join Camdeed, upload your photo, and watch it appear live on the screen.”  
7. “Record someone teaching the youngest guest a trick — bonus if it gets uploaded!”  
8. “Snap a picture of the dessert table before anyone digs in!”

### Why It Works
It makes informal gatherings interactive and memorable. Guests scan a QR, upload media to a shared family album app, and see real-time displays. It emphasizes “family album app” and “wedding photo album” style sharing for any celebration.
