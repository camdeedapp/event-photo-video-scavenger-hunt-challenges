# Corporate & Team-Building Event Challenge Prompts  

**Designed for conferences, team-building events, and company celebrations. Make photo and video sharing effortless with a QR code and live event engagement.**

### Challenge Prompts
1. “Upload your first team photo, and earn points!”  
2. “Record a 10-second ‘elevator pitch’ video with someone you just met.”  
3. “Capture a photo of your team using your company’s core value in action.”  
4. “Take a group video doing a victory pose.”  
5. “Find someone outside your department and snap a selfie with them.”  
6. “Use the event photo album app to upload your shot via QR and watch it appear live on screen.”  
7. “Guess which team will top the leaderboard at the end of the event.”  
8. “Submit a message in the digital guestbook: ‘What’s one thing you’ll take away from today’s event?’”

### Why It Works
It transforms typical corporate gatherings into social games. Guests join via a QR code, upload photos and videos, see their entries live on a big screen, and build a shared album. It also complements “scanner QR code application” and “interactive event software” workflows.
